# Field notes on developer experience

I have spent 7+ years in DevOps and platform engineering, mostly around CI/CD and Kubernetes. This site is where I write down the things I keep running into: the small friction points that slow teams down, what actually helped, and what I would do differently next time.

Nothing here is a tutorial you can copy and paste. It is closer to a working notebook. If something here saves you an afternoon, it has done its job.

[Read the blog](blog/index.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[About me](about.md){ .md-button }

## What I write about

- **CI/CD** – pipelines that developers do not hate, and the ones they do.
- **Kubernetes** – making clusters usable for people who did not sign up to learn Kubernetes.
- **Platform engineering** – internal platforms, golden paths, and the gap between "self-service" on a slide and self-service in practice.
- **Developer experience** – the friction nobody puts on a roadmap because it is too small to name, and too costly to ignore.
